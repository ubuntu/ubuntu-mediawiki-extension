</div></div></includeonly><noinclude>Wraps a source-ordered run of standard [[Template:Card|Card]]
calls in fluid columns. A three-card flow makes its final singleton span the
available width when the flow is in its two-column range.

Set <code>class=ubuntu-card-flow--grid</code> to use a source-ordered grid when
individual cards need explicit spans. A <code>two-wide=yes</code> card spans two
slots at three columns; <code>two-wide-fixed=yes</code> retains two slots at both.

Usage:
<pre>
{{CardFlow|
{{Card|label=...|...}}
{{Card|label=...|...}}
}}
</pre>

For a three-card flow, the final card automatically fills the incomplete
two-column row:
<pre>
{{CardFlow|
{{Card|label=...|...}}
{{Card|label=...|...}}
{{Card|label=...|...}}
}}
</pre>

Featured cards belong directly in [[Template:CardCollection|CardCollection]],
outside a CardFlow.</noinclude>
