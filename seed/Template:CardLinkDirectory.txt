<includeonly>{{#invoke:CardLinkDirectory|list}}</includeonly><noinclude>Renders a reusable directory of navigational links inside a card.
The containing [[Template:Card|Card]] can use <code>link-directory=yes</code> to
adapt its label, description, and link content to the rendered card width.
Empty slots are skipped.

Usage:
<pre>
{{CardLinkDirectory
| [[Getting started with Ubuntu|Installation methods]]
| [[Ubuntu on different platforms|Ubuntu platform reference]]
}}
</pre>

Each item is plain wikitext, written as it would be inside a normal
<code><nowiki><li></nowiki></code>. Positional items are forwarded without a
fixed limit.</noinclude>
