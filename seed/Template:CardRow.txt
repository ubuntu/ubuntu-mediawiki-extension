</div></div></includeonly><noinclude>Wraps cards that share a paired row. At
the widest size the row has three equal slots. Set <code>two-wide-fixed=yes</code>
on one card to make it retain two slots whenever two or more fit. Set
<code>two-wide=yes</code> to span only when three slots fit. The row uses
two equal slots when three <code>18rem</code> minimums no longer fit, then stacks
when two minimums no longer fit.

Usage:
<pre>
{{CardRow|
{{Card|label=Platform-specific wiki pages|wide=yes}}
{{Card|label=Edit the wiki}}
}}
</pre>

Rows collapse naturally when two readable tracks no longer fit.</noinclude>
