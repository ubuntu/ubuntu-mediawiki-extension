<includeonly>{{#invoke:MainPage|main}}</includeonly><noinclude>Renders the fixed main-page card arrangement using caller-provided content.

Each card supports <code>-label</code>, <code>-body</code>, and <code>-content</code>
parameters. Content accepts arbitrary wikitext, including [[Template:ButtonGroup|ButtonGroup]],
[[Template:CardLinkDirectory|CardLinkDirectory]], and admonition templates.

Usage:
<pre>
{{MainPage
| introduction = The Ubuntu wiki makes it easy to find, add and connect ideas about Ubuntu.
| platform-label = Platform-specific wiki pages
| platform-content = {{ButtonGroup|...}}
| edit-label = Edit the wiki
| edit-body = Anyone can contribute.
}}
</pre></noinclude>
