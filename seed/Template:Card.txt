<includeonly>{{#invoke:Card|card|label={{{label|}}}|body={{{body|}}}|content={{{content|}}}|featured={{{featured|}}}|two-wide={{{two-wide|}}}|two-wide-fixed={{{two-wide-fixed|}}}|link-directory={{{link-directory|}}}}}</includeonly><noinclude>Renders an editorial card via [[Module:Card]].

Usage:
<pre>
{{Card
| label = Get started with Ubuntu
| body = Ubuntu is the most popular Linux distro and can be used everywhere.
| content = {{CardLinkDirectory
    | [[Getting started with Ubuntu|Installation methods]]
  }}
}}
</pre>

Parameters:
* '''label''' (required) &ndash; the card's uppercase heading.
* '''body''' (optional) &ndash; an intro paragraph, rendered as wikitext.
* '''content''' (optional) &ndash; extra content below/beside the body, usually a
  nested {{[[Template:ButtonGroup|ButtonGroup]]}} or
  {{[[Template:CardLinkDirectory|CardLinkDirectory]]}} call.
* '''featured''' (optional) &ndash; set to <code>yes</code> to present the card as a
  full-width featured card.
* '''two-wide''' (optional) &ndash; set to <code>yes</code> when the card should occupy
  two slots when the containing flow or row has three slots.
* '''two-wide-fixed''' (optional) &ndash; set to <code>yes</code> when the card should
  retain two slots whenever the containing flow or row has at least two slots.
* '''link-directory''' (optional) &ndash; set to <code>yes</code> when the card pairs
  its label and description with a group of navigational links. The card adapts
  that internal arrangement to its rendered width.</noinclude>
