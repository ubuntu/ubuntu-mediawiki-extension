<includeonly>{{#invoke:HomeCard|card|label={{{label|}}}|body={{{body|}}}|content={{{content|}}}|variant={{{variant|}}}}}</includeonly><noinclude>Renders a homepage section card via [[Module:HomeCard]].

Usage:
<pre>
{{Card
| label = Get started with Ubuntu
| body = Ubuntu is the most popular Linux distro and can be used everywhere.
| content = {{CardList
    | [[Getting started with Ubuntu|Installation methods]]
  }}
}}
</pre>

Parameters:
* '''label''' (required) &ndash; the card's uppercase heading.
* '''body''' (optional) &ndash; an intro paragraph, rendered as wikitext.
* '''content''' (optional) &ndash; extra content below/beside the body, usually a
  nested {{[[Template:ButtonGroup|ButtonGroup]]}} or {{[[Template:CardList|CardList]]}} call.
* '''variant''' (optional) &ndash; space-separated modifiers: <code>col2</code>,
  <code>col3</code>, and/or <code>split</code> (a two-column layout with
  <code>content</code> beside <code>label</code>/<code>body</code>).</noinclude>
