The Ubuntu wiki's shared template styles are produced by on-wiki templates that emit <code>ubuntu-template-*</code> markup. The same templates should render consistently in Vector, Minerva, and the Ubuntu skin.

== Admonitions ==

{{Note|A note gives readers supplementary information without interrupting the surrounding instructions.}}

{{Warning|A warning identifies an action or configuration that needs extra care.}}

{{Tip|A tip suggests a shorter or easier way to complete the task.}}

{{DocCan|A Canonical documentation notice points readers at maintained documentation.}}

{{Info|An information notice highlights context that explains the current page.}}

{{Danger|A danger notice warns about an action that could lose data or break the system.}}
