The seeded Lua modules provide argument handling and reusable card-composition renderers.

* [[Module:Arguments]] — standardizes arguments received by template-facing modules.
* [[Module:ButtonLink]] — renders button-styled wiki and external links.
* [[Module:Card]] — renders editorial cards.
* [[Module:CardComposition]] — composes cards into collections, flows, and rows.
* [[Module:CardLinkDirectory]] — renders card link directories.
* [[Module:MainPage]] — assembles the fixed main-page arrangement used by [[Template:MainPage]].
* [[Module:ScribuntoUnit]] — runs module test suites.
